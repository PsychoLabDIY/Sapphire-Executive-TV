# V6.2 on-device QA

24 November 2026 · Xiaomi TV A Pro 55 (2026), Android TV 14

The installed launcher was inspected using native Android screen captures at 1920 × 1080, not phone photographs. Six visible dock cards measure 252 × 118 px with 24 px gaps; the accessibility bounds show no overlaps or off-screen cards. The home screen, app library, HDMI picker and return to Home were observed. The home clock advanced from 15:35 to 15:38. The translucent tile background is visible through the dock; the native rim is retained.

The owner used the remote during the automated navigation sequence, so the focus-navigation result is inconclusive rather than a regression. The idle clock screen was not reached before the test timeout; its behavior is not certified by this run. Android frame counters are cumulative and not an input-to-photon benchmark. The compositor output does not establish physical panel quality.

The full QA archive, device logs and signing information are not published. Only curated, unmodified native screenshots are intended for the public gallery.
